Akagawa —"the red river"— names what we do: keep a steady, well-directed flow of goods, from source to shelf.

We're a general distribution operation, registered as an LLC, sourcing products for retail partners and building out our own e-commerce presence. Every product we bring in goes through a vetting process for quality, margin, and reliable supply before it joins the catalog.

We're not selling a single house brand — we're selling judgment. The edge is finding the right product, from the right supplier, ahead of the competition.
